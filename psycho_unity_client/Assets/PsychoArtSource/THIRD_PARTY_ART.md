# Psycho Starter Art Sources

This folder contains the first legally safe starter-art pass for Psycho's Unity client. These assets are used as replacement visuals while the project moves from procedural placeholders toward an authored art pipeline.

## Included CC0 Packs

- Quaternius RPG Characters
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-rpg-characters
  - Imported files: selected FBX characters, weapons, and texture PNGs.
  - License: CC0, public domain.

- Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit
  - Imported files: selected FBX trees, rocks, grasses, and texture PNGs.
  - License: CC0, public domain.

- KayKit Medieval Builder Pack 1.0
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/kaykit-medieval-builder-pack-10
  - Imported files: selected FBX buildings and props.
  - License: CC0, public domain.

## Art Direction Notes

The Skyrim screenshots are only mood and quality references: rugged medieval fantasy, readable silhouettes, grounded materials, strong lighting, and cinematic composition. Do not copy Skyrim assets, textures, characters, names, or distinctive designs.

The next production step is to replace these starter packs with a cohesive original/licensed art set as budget allows. Keep every imported pack documented here before it is assigned in the manifest.
